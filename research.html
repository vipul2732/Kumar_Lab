RESEARCH

From molecular mechanisms to systems-scale discovery.

iMBL develops and applies computational approaches spanning molecular simulation, multiscale integrative modeling, AI-enabled drug–target prediction, and in silico discovery of bioactive peptides and natural molecules—connecting structure, dynamics, and function across biological scales.

01

Biomolecular Structure, Dynamics & Membrane Systems

We use molecular modeling and simulations to study protein conformational dynamics, membrane transporters (including SLC systems), blood–brain barrier permeability, and chaperone–client complexes relevant to neurodegeneration and molecular recognition.

02

Data-Driven Multiscale Integrative Modeling

We develop network-informed integrative modeling frameworks that bridge atomic structures, macromolecular assemblies, and systems-level interaction data—including cryo-EM, XL-MS, and AP-MS—within Bayesian platforms such as IMP, connecting structural biology with cellular network context.

03

Deep Learning for Drug–Target & Off-Target Prediction

We build graph-based and deep learning methods that learn binding-site and ligand features to predict drug–target interactions and profile potential off-targets of lead compounds, aiming to improve early-stage drug discovery safety and efficacy.

04

Nutritional Peptide & Natural Product Discovery

We pursue structure-based and AI/ML-enabled discovery of bioactive peptides from Indian millets and pulses, alongside natural-product modulators, using docking, simulations, and Bayesian prioritization for nutrition and therapeutic applications.
