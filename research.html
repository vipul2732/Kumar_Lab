RESEARCH

Understanding biomolecular systems across scales.

LBSD combines structural modeling, simulation, integrative inference, and data-driven approaches to connect molecular architecture with dynamics, interactions, and biological function.

01

Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics

We investigate conformational states, molecular recognition, stability, and dynamics of proteins and biomolecular complexes using computational structural biology and molecular simulations.

02

Integrative & Data-Driven Structural Biology

We aim to combine heterogeneous structural and systems-level information—including cryo-EM, XL-MS, proteomics, interaction networks, comparative models, and AI-based predictions—within probabilistic modeling frameworks.

03

Molecular Discovery & Design

We apply structure-based approaches to characterize ligand recognition and identify molecular modulators, while exploring AI/ML strategies for drug–target interaction and molecular discovery problems.

04

Methods Across Scales

A long-term goal is to develop computational methods that bridge atomic structures, macromolecular assemblies, and systems-level interaction data to generate predictive models of biological organization.
