LBSD opens at JIIT Noida

Vipul Kumar has joined the Department of Biotechnology at Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Noida, as Assistant Professor and is establishing the Laboratory of Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics. Prospective students interested in computational structural biology and molecular discovery are encouraged to get in touch.
