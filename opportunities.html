OPPORTUNITIES

Join iMBL

We welcome motivated students interested in computational structural biology, molecular simulation, integrative modeling, AI/ML for biomolecular systems, and molecular discovery.

01

PhD students

Prospective PhD candidates with a background in biotechnology, bioinformatics, computational biology, physics, chemistry, or related fields are encouraged to write with a CV and a short statement of research interests.

02

Master's students

Master's students interested in dissertation or project work in molecular modeling, simulations, structural bioinformatics, or related computational topics may contact us to discuss possible projects.

Short-term internship and research assistant opportunities may be available depending on ongoing projects and funding. Please email with your background and preferred timeline.
