ABOUT LBSD

Laboratory of Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics

Mission

LBSD investigates how biomolecular structure, dynamics, and molecular interactions govern biological function. We use computational, integrative, and data-driven approaches to uncover molecular mechanisms and enable molecular discovery.

Approach

Our research brings together structural biology, molecular simulation, probabilistic modeling, bioinformatics, and emerging AI/ML methods. We are particularly interested in connecting information across molecular and systems scales.
