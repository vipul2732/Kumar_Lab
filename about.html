ABOUT iMBL

In Silico Molecular Biology Lab

Mission

iMBL investigates biomolecular structure, dynamics, and interactions using computational, integrative, and data-driven approaches. Our work spans multiscale integrative modeling, AI-enabled drug–target prediction, and discovery of bioactive peptides and natural molecules.

Approach

Our research brings together structural biology, molecular simulation, probabilistic modeling, bioinformatics, and emerging AI/ML methods. We are particularly interested in connecting information across molecular and systems scales.
